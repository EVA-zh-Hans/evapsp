## Introduction

*Secret of Evangelion Portable* is a 2007 PSP visual novel in which you play an original character collecting intelligence and uncovering secrets around the Evangelion cast.

## Translation notes

- The patch is distributed as an `xdelta` file and requires the original Japanese ISO
- The repository contains the full toolchain for text extraction, image processing, and patch building

## How to use

1. Download and extract `EVASECRET.xdelta.zip`
2. Apply it to the original Japanese ISO with [Xdelta patcher](https://kotcrab.github.io/xdelta-wasm/)
3. Copy the patched ISO to your PSP or run it in PPSSPP

## Contribute

Development and translation happen in [EVA-zh-Hans/secret_of_eva](https://github.com/EVA-zh-Hans/secret_of_eva). Issues and suggestions are welcome.
