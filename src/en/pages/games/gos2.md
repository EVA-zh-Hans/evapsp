## Introduction

*Neon Genesis Evangelion: Girlfriend of Steel 2nd* is a dating sim developed by GAINAX. This is the 2009 PSP port. The Chinese patch revises and improves the translation originally released by the "EVA Fan Club" (EVA同好会) in 2010.

## Translation notes

- Fixed crashes present in the original Chinese release
- Replaced non-`SJIS` characters 「㈲」「㈹」 with 「有」「代」
- Translated menus and place names
- Revised character name translations
- Adjusted font size
- v1.1 fixes 13 lines of Kaworu's dialogue

## How to use

1. Download the `xdelta` patch for your version
2. Apply it to the original Japanese ISO with [Xdelta patcher](https://kotcrab.github.io/xdelta-wasm/)
3. Copy the patched ISO to your PSP or run it in PPSSPP
