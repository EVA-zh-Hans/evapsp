## Introduction

*Girlfriend of Steel Tokubetsu-Hen* ("Special Edition") was released for PSP on April 9, 2009. The 2009 CG Hanhuazu PSP release was incomplete: inconsistent fonts, visible machine-translation artifacts, and missing lines.

## Translation notes

- Re-translates the story subtitles and dialogue only; UI and system text are not translated
- Subtitle text is based on tehmugi's 2023 English localization
- Text was extracted from textures with Qwen3-VL, translated with DeepSeek V3.2, then manually revised
- Uses the Han Quan Quan Yuan font consistently

## How to use

1. Download the `gos1.xdelta` patch
2. Apply it to the original Japanese ISO with [Xdelta patcher](https://kotcrab.github.io/xdelta-wasm/)
3. Copy the patched ISO to your PSP or run it in PPSSPP
